ClearAuth

Open-source CMS-0057-F prior authorization — FHIR-native intake, auditable adjudication, and SLA enforcement.

View on GitHub ▶ Try the Demo

🧒 What is this? (Plain English)

When your doctor says you need a treatment, your insurance company often has to approve it first — a process called prior authorization that can take days or weeks of faxes and phone calls. ClearAuth is software that does this digitally: the request, the decision rules, and the approval all happen through standard health-data messages, so patients wait minutes instead of weeks.

✨ Key Features

FHIR PAS intake

Prior-auth requests arrive as standard FHIR messages, not faxes.

Auditable adjudication

Every decision step is logged and explainable.

SLA enforcement

Deadlines tracked automatically per CMS-0057-F.

Compliance-first

Built around the US federal interoperability mandate.
