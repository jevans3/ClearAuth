# Medical Necessity Policy: MRI Brain Without Contrast (CPT 70551)

MRI of the brain without contrast is considered medically necessary when the
member has a qualifying neurological diagnosis AND either a documented trial
of conservative therapy has failed, or red-flag symptoms are present that
warrant urgent advanced imaging.

Red-flag symptoms include thunderclap headache onset, new focal neurological
deficit, and papilledema on examination.

Requests not meeting these criteria are pended for clinical review by a
licensed physician reviewer. This policy never authorizes automated denial.
