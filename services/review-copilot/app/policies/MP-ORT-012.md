# Medical Necessity Policy: Total Knee Arthroplasty (CPT 27447)

Total knee arthroplasty is considered medically necessary for members with
primary osteoarthritis of the knee, radiographic evidence of advanced joint
disease (Kellgren-Lawrence grade 3 or 4), and documented failure of at least
three months of conservative management including physical therapy,
intra-articular injections, analgesics, or weight management.

Requests not meeting these criteria are pended for clinical review by a
licensed physician reviewer. This policy never authorizes automated denial.
