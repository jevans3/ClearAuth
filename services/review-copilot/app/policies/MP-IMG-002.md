# Medical Necessity Policy: MRI Lumbar Spine Without Contrast (CPT 72148)

MRI of the lumbar spine without contrast is considered medically necessary
for members with a qualifying lumbar spine diagnosis AND either at least six
weeks of documented conservative therapy (physical therapy, NSAIDs) or a
progressive neurological deficit.

Cauda equina syndrome findings (saddle anesthesia, bowel/bladder dysfunction)
qualify for immediate approval under the progressive-deficit pathway.

Requests not meeting these criteria are pended for clinical review by a
licensed physician reviewer. This policy never authorizes automated denial.
